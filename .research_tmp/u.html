Info

Below you can find a list of file formats that haven't been figured out yet by our senior members.
If you want to help us with them, please let us know in one of the topics listed in Xentax forum link column.

Priotrity:
5 - most important (easiest to figure out)
0 - least important (hardest to figure out)
